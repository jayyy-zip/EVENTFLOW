## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes high-touch bespoke hospitality with the speed, density, and keyboard-first precision of modern engineering tools. It merges the warmth of high-end editorial event production (weddings, galas, milestone celebrations) with the modular efficiency of tools like Linear.

### Emotional Signature
- **Composed & Capable:** Reassures users navigating complex guest lists, vendor contracts, and minute-by-minute run-of-shows.
- **Warm Tactility:** Rejects cold corporate grays in favor of sunlit alabaster, stone, and terracotta undertones.
- **Decisive Intelligence:** Highlights AI copiloting with a dedicated electric indigo violet accent that signals automated insight without overwhelming the canvas.

### Visual Aesthetic
A hybrid of **Warm Editorial Minimalism** and **Linear-Style High-Density Utility**. It relies on crisp 1px borders, subtle surface-tier stepping, hairline dividers, monospace-tuned metrics, and compact, intentional typography to keep data dense, legible, and calming.

## Colors

The palette balances natural, grounded materials with precise functional signals.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAF8F5` (Warm Alabaster) — Soft, ambient foundation that prevents screen fatigue.
- **Card & Modal Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Pure White) — Foreground layers lift sharply against the alabaster base.
- **Subtle Inset / Well Surface:** `#F5F2EC` — Used for table headers, embedded prompt boxes, and active drag targets.
- **Border Default:** `#E7E5E4` (Warm Stone) — Crisp 1px structural framing for all interactive modules.
- **Border Focus & Hover:** `#D6D3D1` — Subtle darkening on interaction.

### Semantic & Accents
- **Primary (`#E05A47` - Terracotta Rose):** Used for primary CTAs, active timeline milestones, critical RSVP confirmations, and brand anchors.
- **Secondary (`#4F46E5` - Electric Indigo):** Dedicated strictly to AI capabilities: copilot recommendations, dynamic schedule re-balancing, guest sentiment summaries, and auto-generated seating plans.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Warm Amber):** Reserved for financial thresholds, payment deadlines, tier warnings, and budget alerts.
- **Text & Content Hierarchy:**
  - Primary Content: `#1C1917` (Deep Warm Slate / Charcoal)
  - Secondary Content: `#78716C` (Muted Stone)
  - Ghost / Placeholder Content: `#A8A29E`

## Typography

Typography establishes structure through scale, contrast, and tight letter spacing.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** brings sculptural, high-end editorial hospitality warmth to display titles, modal headers, and key card summaries.
- **Inter** acts as the high-density workhorse across budget grids, attendee sheets, run-of-show schedules, and copilot prompts. It renders with razor-sharp optical clarity down to 11px.
- Use `mono-metric` styling (leveraging tabular numbers `font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1, 'cv05' 1`) for budget amounts, vendor headcount counters, and minute timestamps to ensure columns align with zero jitter.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an adaptive 12-column responsive grid paired with strict, compact component internal padding.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Split-View Canvas:** Desktop views support a dual-pane or tri-pane layout (Navigation Rail, Event Worktable/Timeline, and an AI Copilot drawer) that expands or contracts smoothly.
- **Rhythm Rules:**
  - Micro-spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) enforces data density inside table cells, chips, and badge groups.
  - Component spacing (`space-md`, `space-lg`) separates cards, section heads, and input groups.
  - Section-level breathing room (`space-xl`) isolates discrete domains (e.g., Run-of-Show vs. Budget Breakdown).

### Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Copilot moves to a slide-up bottom sheet.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Side panels collapse into icon-only triggers.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout, max-width `1600px`, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Full multi-pane workflow with persistent contextual inspectors.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through **warm tonal layering** and **subtle hairline definition**, rather than pronounced drop shadows. This preserves the razor-sharp precision aesthetic while maintaining a soft, natural hand-feel.

### Depth System
1. **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#FAF8F5` background.
2. **Level 1 (Default Containers & Cards):** `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by a 1px `#E7E5E4` line. Shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.03)`.
3. **Level 2 (Hover States & Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a delicate multi-layer ambient shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.03)` and border `#D6D3D1`.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Flyouts):** `#FFFFFF` surface paired with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.07), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04)`.
5. **Level AI (Copilot Floating Shells):** Elevated `#FFFFFF` container with a dual border: 1px internal outline `#4F46E5` at 15% opacity, and a soft indigo-tinted ambient glow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The system uses refined, architectural radiuses that pair snug corners on structural containers with pill geometries on operational metadata tags.

- **Panels, Cards, and Inputs:** Soft corners (`roundedness: 1` = `0.25rem` / `4px` to `rounded-lg: 0.5rem` / `8px`). This maintains an organized, crisp edge alignment across complex data-heavy sheets.
- **Status Badges, Copilot Prompts, and Buttons:** Styled as deliberate pills (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to contrast against rectangular table structures and highlight clickable, interactive items.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#E05A47`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded pill (`9999px`) or `rounded-md` (6px), compact vertical padding (8px 14px), font `Inter 13px weight 500`. Hover: `#D04B38`. Active: `#BA3E2C`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` or transparent base, 1px border `#E7E5E4`, text `#1C1917`. Hover: `#FAF8F5` surface with `#D6D3D1` border.
- **AI Copilot Button:** Deep Indigo `#4F46E5` background with soft lavender highlight or an off-white `#FAF8F5` surface enclosed in a 1px subtle indigo gradient border with an indigo sparkles icon.

### Chips & Pill Tags
- **Dimension:** Height 22px, padding 2px 8px, font size 11px, weight 600, letter spacing `0.02em`.
- **Status (Confirmed / Paid):** Tinted stone-green base (`#F0FDF4`), text `#166534`, 1px border `#DCFCE7`.
- **Alert / Over-budget:** Tinted amber base (`#FFFBEB`), text `#B45309`, 1px border `#FDE68A`.
- **AI Suggested:** Tinted indigo base (`#EEF2FF`), text `#4338CA`, 1px border `#C7D2FE`.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Height 34px (standard density), background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E7E5E4`, radius 6px.
- **Typography:** `Inter` 13px, text `#1C1917`, placeholder `#A8A29E`.
- **States:** Focus rings use an offset 1px crisp outline in `#E05A47` or `#4F46E5` without heavy blurred halos.

### Cards & Data Modules
- **Construction:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#E7E5E4`, radius 8px (`rounded-lg`), padding `space-md` or `space-lg`.
- **Header:** Title in `Plus Jakarta Sans` 14px/16px weight 600, paired with an action slot on the right for quick filters or run-of-show timestamps.

### Lists & Tables
- **Rows:** 36px–40px compact row height, border-bottom 1px `#F5F2EC`, alternating hover highlight of `#FAF8F5`.
- **Cells:** Left-aligned text, right-aligned monetary values and headcounts with tabular figures.

### Specialized AI Copilot Inspector
- Persistent or overlay panel utilizing a pure white surface, top hairline accent in indigo (`#4F46E5`), featuring streaming token animations, quick-action suggestion pills, and structured output previews (e.g., seating map shifts, vendor contract cost comparisons).
